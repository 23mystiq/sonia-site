A music player that feels like an instrument

Glassy waveform display, BPM and key analysis, a bar grid and eight colour cue points saved right into your files.

Mac App StoreComing soon · macOS 15 or later

The waveform is the seek bar

A three-band colour waveform: what has played glows vibrant, what's ahead stays pastel. Click to jump, long-press or pinch to zoom up to 1000%.

BPM, key and bar grid

Detects tempo and musical key (Camelot or classic) for up to four tracks at once, draws a bar grid, and writes BPM and key into your files' tags.

Cue points that stay

Eight colour cue points per track, set with a click or keys 1–8, saved instantly into the file itself in the Serato-compatible cue tag.

Plays what you have

MP3, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, AAC and Apple Lossless. Drop files or whole folders; the playlist appears when you need it.

Seven palettes, light or dark

The display has its own light and dark mode, with seven waveform colour themes and a liquid-glass playhead.

Private and light

No account, no tracking, no internet connection. Your music and data never leave your Mac.
