Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Sonia is a music player for macOS. It is designed to work entirely on your Mac.

No data collection

Sonia does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your music files

Sonia only opens the audio files and folders you choose, by dragging them onto the app or using the Open panel. When you analyse a track or set cue points, Sonia writes the results (tempo, musical key and cue points) into that file's own tags. The audio itself is never modified.

Data kept on your Mac

To work quickly, Sonia keeps a small local database inside its private app container on your Mac: waveform previews, analysis results, cue points and your playlist (as references to the files you added). This data never leaves your Mac. Deleting Sonia removes it.

Children

Sonia does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
