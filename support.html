Support

Sonia 1.0 · macOS 15 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Get help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your macOS version, the file format, and what you did just before the problem.

Frequently asked questions

Which audio formats does Sonia play?

MP3, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, AAC (M4A) and Apple Lossless (ALAC). Drop single files, several files, or whole folders onto the window, or use File ▸ Add Files or Folders… (⌘O).

Does Sonia change my music files?

Only their tags, and only when you analyse a track or set cue points: Sonia writes the BPM, key and cue points into the file. The audio is never modified. If a tag needs more room than the file has, Sonia rewrites the file safely (with a backup it restores if anything is interrupted), and never while that track is playing.

Where are BPM, key and cue points stored?

MP3, WAV and AIFF: in the ID3 tag (TBPM, TKEY, and the "Serato Markers2" cue tag). FLAC: in the Vorbis comments (BPM, INITIALKEY, SERATO_MARKERS_V2). M4A: BPM in the file; key and cue points in Sonia's own library. Sonia also keeps a copy of all analysis results and cue points on your Mac.

Camelot or classic key notation?

Camelot (for example 8A) is the default. Choose classic notation (for example Am) in Sonia ▸ Settings.

How do I zoom the waveform?

Long-click on the waveform, or pinch in on the trackpad (fingers apart), to zoom up to 1000%. Double-click or pinch out (fingers together) to zoom out. Scroll sideways to move around; after a few seconds the view returns to the playhead.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

Space play/pause · ← → previous/next track · ↑ ↓ move through the playlist · Return play the highlighted track · 1–8 set or jump to a cue · A analyse · Delete remove the highlighted cue or the selected tracks.

Are my cue points readable by other DJ software?

Sonia stores cue points in the Serato-compatible "Serato Markers2" tag inside the file. Software that reads this tag can pick them up; support varies between apps and versions.

My playlist disappeared after restarting.

Sonia remembers the playlist between launches. Tracks whose files were moved, renamed elsewhere or deleted can't be reopened and are skipped; add them again from their new location.
